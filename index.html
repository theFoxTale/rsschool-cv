ABOUT ME

Once, I dropped by my friends' workplace to borrow a tent. While we were sitting there, drinking tea and chatting, I accidentally went through a job interview with their company, and they offered me a position as a junior programmer. That's how my career began so unexpectedly.

I'm cheerful, resourceful, love learning, and am good at finding information online. Throughout my work, I've always taught myself a huge amount, but I never received any formal programming training. That's how I grew into a middle fullstack developer and the lead of a small team.

Now I'm interested to see what IT courses are like, which is why I'm here to learn.
